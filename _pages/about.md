Kitsap Housing for All is currently focused on informing local mobile home tenants about the HB 127, new law that prohibits mobile home park owners from raising the rent by more than 5% a year. We also want to give tenants resources if or when their landlords violate this new law. See resources [here](../resources)

We are looking for volunteers to help us canvass at Kitsap mobile home parks to hand out flyers and to let the residents know about their rights. Our next canvasser training session will be November 21st 7pm at Otherwise Society Poulsbo.

**Learn more and get involved [here](../get-involved/).**
